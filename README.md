# MSBA Capstone - Group 1  
## Delivery Strategy Optimization: Identifying Growth-Ready Customers

### Summary of Business Problem and Project Objective

Swire Coca-Cola (SCCU) aims to optimize its delivery logistics by transitioning low-volume customers to cost-effective third-party delivery services, known as Alternate Routes to Market (ARTM), facilitated by white trucks. While this shift reduces operational costs, it poses a risk: **high-potential customers may be misclassified**, leading to lost growth opportunities and weakened relationships due to reduced service touchpoints.

**Project Objective:**  
Develop a data-driven strategy to **identify and protect growth-ready customers**, ensuring that red truck (high-touch) delivery is prioritized for the most valuable accounts.

---

### Our Group’s Solution

To address this challenge, we:

- Explored and evaluated **20+ advanced machine learning models**
- Engineered key features from over 1M transactional records and 30K+ customer profiles
- Developed a **dynamic Tableau dashboard** for interactive decision-making

Our solution empowers Swire Coca-Cola to:

- **Run real-time ‘what-if’ analyses** to evaluate delivery strategies  
- **Segment customers** based on purchasing patterns and growth potential  
- **Predict high-potential customers** currently assigned to ARTM  
- **Automate truck assignments** to maximize cost-efficiency without sacrificing future revenue
